### Form & Questionnaire Design Rule
- **Use Step-by-Step Wizards**: When building questionnaires or multi-part forms, default to a modern step-by-step wizard (one question/section per view) rather than a long scrolling page.
- **Rich Visuals**: Use colorful icons for categories and options.
- **Animations**: Implement micro-animations (e.g., slide-ins, fade transitions between steps) to make the form feel interactive and alive.
- **Glassmorphism**: When using glass effects, ensure they are strong and distinct (deep blurs, subtle borders) to feel premium.
